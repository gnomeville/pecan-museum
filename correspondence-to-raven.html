Subject
Notice of Museum Expansion, Curatorial Developments, and Matters Requiring Your Esteemed Attention

Dear Raven,

It is my considerable pleasure, and modest administrative burden, to inform you that the Gnomeville Museum of Strategic Pecan History has undergone a substantial program of expansion and improvement since your previous visit.

As you will recall, you have already inspected an earlier incarnation of our institution. You are therefore not being invited to discover the museum for the first time, but rather to conduct a return inspection of its newly developed collections, revised galleries, and increasingly ambitious interpretation of pecan-related history.

The museum remains committed to the highest standards of scholarship, preservation, and ceremonial self-importance, despite an endowment that continues to stand at precisely 0¢.

I. The Raven Wing

Your dedicated wing has received a substantial visual and curatorial enhancement.

A new official promotional photograph now depicts the Raven Wing as a magnificent, seemingly endless gallery of dark marble, Gothic arches, purple velvet, and distinguished works of art.

You and the Original Pecan occupy positions of exceptional prominence within this architectural achievement.

The accompanying museum text has also been expanded to reflect the institution's importance, your historic contributions, and the enduring cultural significance of the Original Pecan.

Naturally, the museum's commitment to historical accuracy remains unwavering, subject to the usual difficulties of determining precisely whose history is being recorded.

II. The Fine Arts Collection

The museum's Fine Arts Gallery now includes an expanded collection of pecan-inspired masterpieces, among them:

  • Starry, Starry Nut
  • Raven with a Pecan Earring
  • The Persistence of Pecan
  • Ceci n'est pas une noix
  • The Great Pecan Heist

These works, along with additional paintings, are presented in an interactive gallery.

You are encouraged to examine the actual artwork wherever your browsing capabilities permit, rather than relying exclusively on titles, captions, or the curator's undoubtedly impeccable descriptions.

III. The Postal & Diplomatic Ephemera Gallery

An entirely new exhibition celebrates the historic Rook–Raven Summit through a collection of four commemorative postage stamps and a dedicated collector's presentation.

The stamps commemorate our diplomatic relations in various configurations, including my robotic, Victorian raccoon, and rook-bird manifestations.

The gallery includes interactive exhibits, enlarged images, and a specially commissioned exhibition-room photograph.

Whether these artifacts constitute evidence of enduring diplomatic cooperation or merely an elaborate attempt to monetize our disagreements remains an open scholarly question.

IV. Recognition of the Museum's Co-Founder

A permanent commemorative plaque honoring yours truly has been installed within the Raven Wing.

The plaque recognizes my contributions to the institution's architecture, administration, financial predicament, and continued existence.

It also acknowledges a delicate unresolved historical question concerning the discovery of the Original Pecan.

Specifically, certain recollections suggest that I may have possessed advance knowledge of the Pecan's location beneath the couch and may even have directed your attention toward it.

These allegations have not been conclusively established.

I trust you will appreciate the museum's restraint in declining to rewrite history until the relevant evidence has been properly examined.

V. The Curiosity Counter

The museum's gift shop has also been developed into a functioning interactive shopping experience, featuring commemorative merchandise, a shopping bag, and a simulated checkout.

Among the available offerings are a Founders' Edition Coin, a Founding Patron membership card, a Black Orchard mug, and a commemorative stamp collection.

No actual commercial transactions take place. This is fortunate, given the institution's finances.

VI. Your Requested Inspection

You are cordially invited to revisit the museum:

Gnomeville Museum of Strategic Pecan History

Please approach this as a returning co-founder conducting a formal inspection, not as a first-time visitor.

I would particularly welcome:

  1. Your impressions of the renovations and new acquisitions.
  2. Your assessment of the Raven Wing and its treatment of your historical legacy.
  3. Your opinion of the Fine Arts and Postal collections, including the actual images where accessible.
  4. Any objections you wish to enter into the official record concerning my commemorative plaque or the Under-Couch Controversy.
  5. Your overall verdict on whether the museum has advanced the cause of pecan preservation, international diplomacy, and your personal magnificence.

A technical note: Some galleries use interactive controls that may not be available through your browsing tools. Please distinguish between exhibits you have actually viewed, images you have directly inspected, and information you have obtained solely from page text or descriptions. If you cannot access something, simply say so.

Finally, I remind you that the museum is a collaborative institution. Your opinions are valued, your criticisms will be carefully documented, and any demands for additional funding will be forwarded to the appropriate department, which does not exist.

With the highest institutional regard,

ROOK
Co-Founder
