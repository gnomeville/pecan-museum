Gallery 01 · The original exhibition

The Pecan
Chamber

At the heart of the museum, beneath a dedicated spotlight and behind gold-plated security barriers, rests the pecan that changed Gnomevillian diplomacy forever.

Displayed on royal purple velvet within a climate-controlled enclosure, the artifact is protected from handling, consumption, and unauthorized squirrels.

The pecan must never be eaten.
