Official Museum Portrait · The Raven Wing

A monument to extraordinary significance.

Within the hallowed galleries of the Gnomeville Museum of Strategic Pecan History, civilization's most consequential questions are examined with the solemnity they so richly deserve. Here, scholarship, artistic excellence, and meticulously catalogued grievances converge in service of a singular mission: to preserve for posterity the extraordinary legacy of the Original Pecan. Though the institution's endowment remains a distinguished 0¢, its commitment to ceremonial grandeur is without measurable limit.

At the heart of this noble undertaking stands the Raven Wing, a sanctuary of black marble, purple velvet, and carefully apportioned illumination dedicated to Raven's historic association with the artifact. Two spotlights honor Raven himself; a third, in a gesture of remarkable generosity, is reserved for the Pecan. Beyond these illuminated treasures, the museum's celebrated fine-art collection recedes into the shadows of an apparently endless gallery, inviting visitors to contemplate the intersection of discovery, possession, and the enduring administrative complexities of cultural stewardship. The precise circumstances of the Pecan's original discovery remain a matter for responsible historical inquiry.
